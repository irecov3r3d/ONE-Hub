# ⚡ Hub Synergy Audit: Song Generator Pro Hub

This audit defines the definitive mission and collaborative architecture of the Song Generator Pro Hub, categorizing all existing branches into a synergistic pipeline or designating them for relocation.

## 🎵 The Song Generator Pro Hub Mission
To create an integrated, AI-driven music production ecosystem where organic capture, intelligent generation, and professional refinement collaborate seamlessly.

### 🔄 Collaborative Workflow
1. **Capture**: Organic ideas are captured via the **Voice Recorder** or rhythmic foundations are built in the **Beat Maker**.
2. **Generation**: The **Ensemble AI** (Multi-Model Service) generates high-fidelity audio based on user prompts and captured ideas.
3. **Refinement**: Generated audio is analyzed by the **AudioAnalysisService** for quality metrics, which then informs the **AudioMasteringService** for professional polishing (EQ, Compression, Limiting).
4. **Storage**: Final masters are securely stored and managed in the **Music Vault**.
5. **Presentation**: Results are visualized using **Auto-Split Video Clips** and dynamic visualizers for social sharing.
