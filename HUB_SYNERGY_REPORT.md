## 🤝 Collaborative Workflow & Synergy

The **Song Generator Pro Hub** operates as a high-performance pipeline where each specialized service feeds the next to ensure professional-grade output:

1.  **CAPTURE (The Seed)**:
    -   Organic vocal/instrumental ideas are captured via the **Voice Recorder**.
    -   Rhythmic foundations or MIDI patterns are established in the **Beat Maker**.
    -   *Synergy*: These provide the grounding constraints for the AI generation engine.

2.  **GENERATION (The Engine)**:
    -   The **Core AI Ensemble** (`main`) processes seeds from the Capture phase to generate high-fidelity multi-track compositions.
    -   *Synergy*: Uses `FastFFTEngine` for real-time validation of spectral density during generation.

3.  **REFINEMENT (The Studio)**:
    -   **Audio Analysis Service**: Performs deep inspection of the generated audio (Loudness, Dynamic Range, Stereo Width, Spectral Balance).
    -   **Audio Mastering Service**: Uses metrics from Analysis to apply targeted EQ, Compression, and Limiting to meet industry standards (e.g., -14 LUFS).
    -   *Synergy*: Analysis metrics drive the automated mastering parameters, ensuring a "closed-loop" quality guarantee.

4.  **STORAGE (The Vault)**:
    -   **Music Vault**: Provides persistent storage, versioning, and metadata indexing for both raw generations and mastered finals.
    -   *Synergy*: Tracks the lineage of a song from capture to final master.

5.  **PRESENTATION (The Stage)**:
    -   **Auto Video Splitter**: Generates reactive visuals, waveforms, and social-ready clips from the mastered assets.
    -   *Synergy*: Finalizes the workflow by preparing the music for distribution and engagement.
