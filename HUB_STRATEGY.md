# Song Generator Pro Hub: Collaborative Strategy

The Song Generator Pro Hub is an integrated ecosystem designed to take musical ideas from inception to professional-grade final products.

## Core Collaborative Pipeline

1.  **Creation & Capture**:
    - **Voice Recorder** (`codex/create-advanced-voice-recorder-app`): Captures raw vocal ideas.
    - **Beat Maker** (`claude/beat-maker-app-Fhpg2`): Provides rhythmic foundations.
2.  **AI Generation**:
    - **Song Generator** (`claude/song-generator-T7GUx`): Uses multi-model ensembles to expand ideas into full compositions.
3.  **Quality Assurance & Optimization**:
    - **Audio Analysis Service** (`lib/services/audioAnalysisService.ts`): Provides deep technical metrics (LUFS, dynamic range, spectral balance, key detection).
    - **Audio Mastering Service** (`lib/services/audioMasteringService.ts`): Applies professional DSP (EQ, Compression, Limiting) to meet industry standards.
4.  **Distribution & Storage**:
    - **Music Vault** (`claude/music-vault-app-DNlEb`): Centralized library for asset management.
    - **Video Visualizer** (`claude/auto-split-video-clips-gwIQz`): Automates content creation for social media sharing.

## Performance as a Core Feature

To maintain a seamless "Pro" experience, the hub prioritizes:
- **Low Latency**: Real-time analysis and fast mastering turnaround.
- **Efficiency**: Minimizing memory pressure during high-resolution audio processing to support long tracks and batch operations.
- **Accuracy**: Reliable musical and technical metrics to inform both the AI generation and the mastering chain.
