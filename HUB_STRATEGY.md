# ⚡ Bolt Hub Strategy: Song Generator Pro

## 🎵 Song Generator Pro Hub (Keep)
The Song Generator Pro Hub is an integrated AI-driven music production ecosystem. These branches collaborate to provide a complete workflow from initial idea to professional master.

### Collaborative Ecosystem:
1.  **Capture (Organic Input)**:
    - `codex/add-mvp-features-for-voice-recorder`
    - `codex/create-advanced-voice-recorder-app`
    - *Purpose*: Capturing vocal performances, acoustic instruments, or environmental samples as raw material for generation or processing.
2.  **Generation (Ensemble AI)**:
    - `main` / `claude/song-generator-T7GUx`
    - `claude/beat-maker-app-Fhpg2`
    - *Purpose*: Utilizing multi-model AI (MusicGen, Riffusion, etc.) and MIDI generation to create musical foundations from text prompts or captured audio.
3.  **Refinement (Analysis & Mastering)**:
    - `claude/audio-analysis-mastering-tool-ySQzQ`
    - *Purpose*: Professional finishing via `AudioMasteringService` (EQ, compression, limiting) and quality validation via `AudioAnalysisService` (LUFS, spectrum, dynamic range).
4.  **Storage (Music Vault)**:
    - `claude/music-vault-app-DNlEb`
    - *Purpose*: Centralized asset management, versioning, and cloud storage for project files and final masters.
5.  **Presentation (Visualizers)**:
    - `claude/auto-split-video-clips-gwIQz`
    - *Purpose*: Automated generation of spectral visualizers and video content for social sharing.
