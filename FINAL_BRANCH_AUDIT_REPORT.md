## 🤝 Hub Synergy & Workflow
The Song Generator Pro Hub operates as an integrated ecosystem where specialized services collaborate to take a musical idea from inception to presentation:

### Functional Synergy Mapping
1. **Organic Capture / Voice Recorder** (`codex/create-advanced-voice-recorder-app`)
   - **Role**: Captures high-fidelity, organic audio signals (vox/instrument/ambient) from user mic.
   - **Data Flow**: Outputs raw PCM `.wav` blobs representing the user's vocal sketches or instrument lines.
   - **Downstream Collaboration**: Passes raw PCM files to **Core AI Generation** as creative prompt/seed audio, and to **Audio Analysis** to inspect recording quality (noise floor, clipping).

2. **Rhythmic Foundation / Beat Maker** (`claude/beat-maker-app-Fhpg2`)
   - **Role**: Allows structural MIDI/rhythm generation, grid-based sequencing, and pattern selection.
   - **Data Flow**: Generates synthesized rhythm stems, BPM coordinates, and timing metadata (syncopation/onsets).
   - **Downstream Collaboration**: Transmits BPM/grid alignment settings to **Core AI Generation** to lock synthesis loops, and passes structural audio loops directly to the **Audio Analysis Service** to confirm precise time signature and tempo alignment.

3. **Core AI Generation** (`claude/song-generator-T7GUx`)
   - **Role**: Coordinates multi-model generative processes to translate text prompts, organic seeds, and rhythms into complete musical structures.
   - **Data Flow**: Combines vocal recordings and beat files, outputs raw multi-track audio compositions.
   - **Downstream Collaboration**: Passes synthesized output buffers to the **Audio Analysis Service** for automated quality, dynamic range, and key/scale validation.

4. **Refinement & Studio Polish / Audio Analysis & Mastering** (`claude/audio-analysis-mastering-tool-ySQzQ`)
   - **Role**: Validates, corrects, and finishes the audio generated by the AI model.
   - **Data Flow**:
     - **AudioAnalysisService** parses the incoming buffer, calculates features (LUFS, Crest Factor, Stereo Width, Pitch Distribution, Spectral Flatness) and produces detailed JSON/metadata reporting.
     - **AdvancedKeyDetection** runs template matching against Krumhansl-Schmuckler profiles to identify musical tonality.
     - **AudioMasteringService** applies professional-grade DSP effects (compressor, EQ, saturator, limiter) based on analysis suggestions to target a standardized -14 LUFS level.
   - **Downstream Collaboration**: Stores fully finished audio files into the **Music Vault** and provides pre-calculated spectral magnitudes directly to the **Presentation Waveform Editor** for instantaneous visual rendering without re-analysis.

5. **Presentation & Storage** (`claude/music-vault-app-DNlEb` & `claude/auto-split-video-clips-gwIQz`)
   - **Role**: Manages asset preservation, cataloging, and promotional visual generation.
   - **Data Flow**:
     - **Music Vault** indexes wav/mp3 metadata, providing persistence and searchability.
     - **Auto Video Splitter & Waveform Editor** consume refined audio and pre-calculated spectrum vectors to draw custom interactive waveforms and render social-ready promotional visualizers.
