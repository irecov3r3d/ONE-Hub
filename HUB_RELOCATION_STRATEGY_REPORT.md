## 🎵 Song Generator Pro Hub Synergy
The remaining core branches form a highly integrated music production ecosystem. Each component plays a vital role in the lifecycle of a musical asset:

1.  **Capture (Organic Seeds)**:
    - *Branches*: `voice-recorder-app`, `beat-maker-app`
    - *Role*: Provides the initial rhythmic or melodic inspiration. Capture organic audio or quantized beats.

2.  **Generation (The AI Engine)**:
    - *Branches*: `main`, `song-generator-T7GUx`
    - *Role*: Takes seeds from Capture and uses multi-model ensembles to generate full instrumental tracks.

3.  **Refinement (The Studio)**:
    - *Branches*: `audio-analysis-mastering-tool`, `bolt/*`
    - *Role*: Validates audio quality (Analysis) and applies professional-grade DSP (Mastering) to ensure the output is ready for distribution.

4.  **Storage (The Vault)**:
    - *Branches*: `music-vault-app`
    - *Role*: Securely archives finalized assets with metadata for easy retrieval and sharing.

5.  **Presentation (The Stage)**:
    - *Branches*: `auto-split-video-clips`
    - *Role*: Transforms audio into social-ready video content through synchronized splitting and waveform visualization.
