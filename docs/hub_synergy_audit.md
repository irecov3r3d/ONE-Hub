# ⚡ Hub Synergy Audit & Branch Relocation

## 🔄 Core Hub Workflow
The Song Generator Pro Hub operates as an integrated AI-driven music production ecosystem:
1. **Capture**: Organic audio capture via Voice Recorder and rhythmic foundation via Beat Maker.
2. **Generation**: Multi-model Ensemble AI generates musical content based on prompts and captured assets.
3. **Refinement**: Professional-grade Audio Analysis and Mastering services ensure high quality and consistency.
4. **Storage**: Music Vault provides persistent asset management and library storage.
5. **Presentation**: Visualizers and Video Clip generators create engaging content for the produced music.
